# 📦 Inventory Management & Business Intelligence Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Inventory Management & Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** for ToyJoy. The dashboard analyzes inventory performance, seasonal demand, stock availability, overstock situations, stockout risks, and inventory turnover across multiple product categories. The objective is to optimize inventory levels, reduce holding costs, improve stock availability, and support data-driven supply chain decisions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify products with high overstock and low sales.
- Analyze seasonal demand patterns for inventory planning.
- Optimize inventory turnover and stock allocation.
- Reduce stockout risk for high-demand products.
- Improve inventory efficiency through strategic reorder planning. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## 📈 Key Insights

- Inventory turnover reached **161.05**, while the overall stockout rate remained only **2%**, indicating efficient inventory movement with minimal shortages.
- Off-season recorded the highest inventory levels, while Christmas experienced the strongest sales intensity despite lower overall volume.
- Mega Warrior and Chess Master generated the highest excess inventory value, locking significant working capital.
- Action Figures contributed the highest excess inventory value, whereas Puzzles carried the largest stock volume.
- Space Ranger, Toddler Pal, Princess Dream, and Ludo King consistently ranked among the highest-selling products.
- Space Ranger experienced the highest stockout rate, indicating strong customer demand and the need for higher reorder points.
- Overstock steadily declined across the three years, reflecting continuous improvement in inventory planning and management. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase reorder points for high-demand products such as Space Ranger, Ludo King, Ninja Strike, and Snake & Ladder.
- Reduce purchase quantities for slow-moving products including Mega Warrior and Chess Master to lower inventory holding costs.
- Introduce seasonal inventory planning based on historical demand patterns.
- Build a demand forecasting model using three years of sales data to improve inventory accuracy.
- Implement minimum-maximum stock policies and automated reorder triggers to reduce stockouts while maintaining optimal inventory levels.
- Run clearance campaigns for overstocked products before peak festive seasons to free warehouse capacity. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
